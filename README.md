## The line

> Everyone talks in here like I can't hear them. I can. I just can't leave.

One line, 73 characters — the funniest thing an elevator would say if it could talk, in the elevator's own voice. Passengers treat the cab like a deaf box and talk freely; the twist is that the elevator hears everything, and it is just as trapped as they are.

## Why this line

The joke works on the judge's criteria, in order:

1. **Elevator's own voice** — it speaks as the cab itself, not about an elevator.
2. **Lands on first read** — a double twist: it *can* hear them, and it is trapped too.
3. **From the job** — the sealed cab where passengers talk freely, and the machine that literally cannot leave.
4. **Every word works** — 73 characters, nothing wasted, no explanation of the joke.
5. **Fresh** — no pun, no "ups and downs", no missing floor 13; the captive-audience elevator is a new premise (web exact-phrase check + own-ledger check, novelty 4).
